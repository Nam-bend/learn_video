# 3.2.7. Thiet ke co so du lieu vat ly (Physical Database Design)

He thong su dung he quan tri co so du lieu quan he PostgreSQL ket hop voi extension pgvector đe luu tru va tim kiem vector. Duoi đay la đac ta chi tiet cac bang vat ly.

# 1. Bang users (Quan ly tai khoan nguoi dung)

Luu tru thong tin đinh danh va bao mat cua nguoi hoc.

|Ten cot|Kieu du lieu|Khoa|Bat buoc (Not<br>Null)|Mo ta|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|id|UUID|PK|X|Khoa chinh,<br>ma đinh danh<br>nguoi dung|
|email|VARCHAR(255)|UNIQUE|X|Đia chi email<br>đang nhap<br>(Duynhat)|
|hashed_password|VARCHAR(255)||X|Mat khau đa<br>đuoc ma hoa<br>bangbcrypt|
|created_at|TIMESTAMP||X|Thoi gian tao<br>tai khoan|

|---|---|---|---|---|
|media_type|VARCHAR(50)||X|Loai media<br>(video, pdf,<br>docx)|
|status|VARCHAR(50)||X|Trang thai xu<br>ly (pending,<br>transcribing,<br>done, error)|
